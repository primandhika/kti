## 3. Apakah Datanya Masih Terlihat Fishy?

Secara umum, data sekarang tidak tampak fishy. Justru versi revisi sudah jauh lebih masuk akal karena ada penjelasan denominator dan fluktuasi session-level.

### 3.1 Data mean utama

Data mean sudah cukup masuk akal untuk skala 1 sampai 3:

| Indikator | Pre | Post | Catatan |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Planning | 1.42 | 2.68 | Peningkatan besar, tetapi masih masuk akal untuk intervensi berbasis rutinitas. |
| Monitoring | 1.36 | 2.51 | Peningkatan masuk akal dan tidak terlalu sempurna. |
| Reflection | 1.18 | 2.74 | Peningkatan paling besar, sesuai logika karena refleksi lebih mudah diprompt setelah speaking. |
| Overall | 1.32 | 2.64 | Konsisten dengan rata-rata tiga indikator. |

Data ini tidak otomatis mencurigakan. Namun, agar reviewer tidak meragukan angka tersebut, pastikan:

- SD tetap ditampilkan.
- Skala 1 sampai 3 dijelaskan dengan indikator operasional.
- Penilai atau observer dijelaskan.
- Keterbatasan single observer tetap diakui.
- Tidak ada klaim inferensial berlebihan.

### 3.2 Data persentase perilaku

Persentase seperti 51.79% dan 66.07% sebelumnya terlihat janggal kalau denominator-nya 28 siswa. Setelah dijelaskan sebagai 56 *student-session observations* pada Sessions 1-2 dan Sessions 7-8, angka tersebut menjadi masuk akal.

Contoh logika:

- 51.79% dari 56 observasi = sekitar 29 observasi.
- 66.07% dari 56 observasi = sekitar 37 observasi.

Ini aman, asalkan narasi tabel tidak menyebutnya sebagai “percentage of students”.

### Redaksi yang disarankan

Gunakan:

> The percentages represent the proportion of student-session observations in which the target behavior was observed across two-session blocks.

Jangan gunakan:

> The percentages represent the proportion of students who demonstrated the behavior.

## 4.3 Klaim masih harus konsisten diturunkan

Karena desainnya single-group descriptive pre-post, hindari diksi yang terlalu kuat.

### Kata yang perlu dihindari

- proves
- confirms
- demonstrates effectiveness
- effective intervention
- caused improvement
- significantly improved, kecuali ada uji statistik yang benar
- impact, jika dimaknai kausal

### Kata yang lebih aman

- suggests
- indicates
- was associated with
- provides descriptive evidence
- points to
- appeared to support
- was observed alongside
- emerging classroom process

### Contoh revisi redaksi

Kurang aman:

> This study demonstrates that digital microlearning effectively improves students’ metacognitive awareness in speaking.

Lebih aman:

> This study provides descriptive evidence that repeated digital microlearning prompts were associated with the gradual emergence of metacognitive speaking behaviors in a primary classroom context.

Kurang aman:

> The intervention caused students to become more reflective speakers.

Lebih aman:

> The intervention appeared to support more frequent reflective behaviors during repeated speaking routines.

## 7. Rekomendasi Redaksi untuk Abstract

Gunakan abstract yang sangat hati-hati dan tidak berlebihan.

### Versi redaksi yang disarankan

> **Background/purpose.** Primary-level speaking instruction often emphasizes fluency and accuracy while providing limited opportunities for learners to regulate speaking through planning, monitoring, and reflection. Although metacognitive awareness has been widely examined in reading and writing, evidence on its habituation in primary students’ speaking remains limited. This study examined how metacognitive awareness was habituated in primary students’ speaking practices through digital microlearning as a routine classroom scaffold.
>
> **Materials/methods.** A classroom-based, process-oriented intervention was conducted using a single-group descriptive pre-post design with qualitative triangulation. Twenty-eight Grade 5 students participated in eight speaking sessions over four weeks. Digital microlearning prompts were embedded before and after speaking tasks to support planning, monitoring, and reflection. Data were collected through observation rubrics, student reflections, and teacher observation notes, and were analyzed descriptively and thematically.
>
> **Results.** The findings indicated a gradual increase in observable metacognitive speaking behaviors. Overall metacognitive awareness scores increased from 1.32 to 2.64, with gains observed in planning, monitoring, and post-speaking reflection. Session-level observations also showed increased occurrences of pausing to plan, self-correction during speaking, and voluntary reflection. Qualitative evidence suggested a shift from surface-level evaluation toward more process-oriented self-regulation.
>
> **Conclusion.** The study provides descriptive evidence that repeated digital microlearning prompts can support the habituation of metacognitive awareness in primary students’ speaking routines. The findings should be interpreted as evidence of an emerging classroom process rather than causal effectiveness, given the single-group design and limited sample size.

---

## 8. Rekomendasi Redaksi untuk Limitation

Bagian limitation perlu jujur, tetapi jangan terlalu melemahkan artikel.

### Versi redaksi yang disarankan

> This study has several limitations. First, it involved one intact Grade 5 classroom and used a single-group descriptive pre-post design; therefore, the findings should not be interpreted as evidence of causal effectiveness. Second, the intervention was conducted over four weeks, which limits claims about long-term transfer or sustained metacognitive habituation. Third, classroom observations relied primarily on one observer, although triangulation with student reflections and teacher notes was used to strengthen interpretation. Future studies should involve larger and more diverse samples, comparison-group designs, multiple raters, and longer follow-up periods to examine whether reflective speaking routines are sustained beyond the intervention context.

---

## 9. Rekomendasi Redaksi untuk Conclusion

Conclusion perlu menegaskan kontribusi tanpa klaim berlebihan.

### Versi redaksi yang disarankan

> This study suggests that metacognitive awareness in primary students’ speaking can be gradually habituated through repeated reflective routines supported by concise digital microlearning prompts. Across eight speaking sessions, students showed increased observable behaviors related to planning before speaking, monitoring during speaking, and reflecting after speaking. Qualitative evidence also indicated a shift from brief evaluative comments toward more process-oriented reflection. These findings contribute to primary speaking pedagogy by showing how metacognitive regulation can be embedded into ordinary classroom speaking routines without requiring lengthy explicit instruction. Given the single-group design and short intervention period, the findings should be understood as descriptive evidence of an emerging classroom process rather than as proof of intervention effectiveness.
