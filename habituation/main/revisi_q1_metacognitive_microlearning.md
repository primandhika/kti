## 1. Verdict Umum

Artikel ini sudah memiliki topik yang cukup kuat, yaitu pengembangan kesadaran metakognitif dalam praktik berbicara siswa SD melalui digital microlearning. Secara konseptual, kombinasi antara speaking pedagogy, metacognitive awareness, habituation, dan microlearning memiliki potensi kontribusi yang baik.

Namun, angka tersebut masuk akal jika denominator-nya adalah **56 student-session observations**, yaitu 28 siswa × 2 sesi untuk early sessions dan 28 siswa × 2 sesi untuk final sessions.

### Risiko Reviewer

Reviewer dapat menganggap data ini janggal atau fishy karena persentasenya tidak sesuai dengan jumlah peserta. Ini bukan berarti datanya palsu, tetapi penjelasan denominator-nya wajib diperbaiki.

### Tindakan Revisi

Jangan menyebut data ini sebagai “percentage of students”. Ubah menjadi “percentage of student-session observations”.

### Redaksi yang Disarankan untuk Metode

> Session-level behavioral data were calculated based on student-session observations. Early sessions referred to Sessions 1 and 2, producing 56 observation units from 28 students across two sessions. Final sessions referred to Sessions 7 and 8, also producing 56 observation units. Percentages therefore represent the proportion of student-session observations in which each target behavior was observed, rather than the proportion of individual students.

### Redaksi yang Disarankan untuk Judul Tabel

Sebelumnya:

Disarankan:

## 6. Masalah Instrumen Observasi

### Masalah

Instrumen hanya disebut sebagai “researcher-developed observation rubric”. Belum ada informasi tentang:

1. jumlah indikator,
2. definisi skor 1 sampai 3,
3. contoh perilaku untuk setiap skor,
4. proses validasi ahli,
5. siapa observer,
6. apakah ada second rater,
7. inter-rater agreement,
8. kapan pre dan post diukur.

### Risiko Reviewer

Reviewer Q1 biasanya akan menuntut auditabilitas instrumen. Tanpa penjelasan ini, data kuantitatif terlihat lemah.

### Tindakan Revisi

Tambahkan subbagian khusus di metode: **Observation Rubric and Scoring Procedure**.

### Redaksi yang Disarankan

> The observation rubric was developed to capture three metacognitive speaking indicators: planning before speaking, monitoring during speaking, and reflection after speaking. Each indicator was rated on a three-point scale: 1 = not observed or only externally prompted, 2 = partially observed with teacher support, and 3 = consistently observed with increasing student initiative. The rubric was reviewed by two experts in language education and classroom assessment to ensure developmental appropriateness and alignment with the study constructs. Prior to the intervention, the observer and classroom teacher discussed the scoring criteria using sample classroom behaviors to reduce interpretive ambiguity.

Jika ada dua penilai:

> A subset of observation records was independently rated by two raters. Inter-rater agreement was calculated to examine scoring consistency before the final dataset was analyzed descriptively.

Jika belum ada dua penilai:

> Because the study relied on a single primary observer, the findings should be interpreted as descriptive classroom evidence rather than as independently verified behavioral measurement. To reduce observer bias, observation notes were triangulated with teacher notes and student reflections.

### Contoh Rubrik yang Bisa Dimasukkan

| Indicator | Score 1 | Score 2 | Score 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planning before speaking | Student begins speaking without visible preparation or only responds after teacher direction. | Student shows brief preparation, such as pausing or organizing one idea, but still depends on teacher prompts. | Student independently pauses, organizes ideas, or states what they intend to say before speaking. |
| Monitoring during speaking | Student continues speaking without noticing unclear expression or relies entirely on teacher correction. | Student occasionally notices unclear expression and makes limited corrections with teacher support. | Student independently self-corrects, rephrases, or adjusts clarity while speaking. |
| Reflection after speaking | Student gives only affective or evaluative comments, such as “good” or “difficult”. | Student identifies one aspect of performance with teacher guidance. | Student identifies what worked, what was unclear, and what to improve in the next speaking task. |

## 8. Masalah Data Kualitatif yang Terlalu Rapi

### Masalah

Bagian hasil menyebut:

> Across the sampled reflections, 10 excerpts were coded as general evaluation, 10 as planning awareness, 10 as monitoring awareness, and 10 as reflective evaluation.

Distribusi 10-10-10-10 terlihat terlalu simetris. Ini dapat menimbulkan kesan bahwa data dipilih agar tampak rapi, bukan muncul secara alami dari proses coding.

### Risiko Reviewer

Reviewer dapat mempertanyakan:

2. apakah 40 kutipan itu seluruh data atau sampel,
3. bagaimana kutipan dipilih,
4. apakah kategori bersifat mutually exclusive,
5. apakah coding dilakukan oleh satu atau dua coder,

### Tindakan Revisi

### Redaksi yang Disarankan

> Student reflections were analyzed thematically to examine changes in the quality of metacognitive expression. Rather than treating the excerpts as frequency data, the qualitative analysis focused on representative patterns across the intervention. Early reflections were generally affective and evaluative, whereas later reflections increasingly referred to planning, monitoring, and improvement strategies.

Tambahkan prosedur:

> The qualitative dataset consisted of students' written reflections collected across the intervention sessions and teacher observation notes. Reflections were first read repeatedly to identify recurring patterns. Initial codes were then grouped into broader categories corresponding to general evaluation, planning awareness, monitoring awareness, and reflective evaluation. Representative excerpts were selected to illustrate each pattern. When excerpts were translated from Indonesian into English, the translation preserved the meaning of students' original expressions rather than producing literal word-for-word equivalents.

## 16. Masalah Abstrak

### Masalah

Abstrak sudah rapi, tetapi terlalu kuat pada klaim hasil. Selain itu, belum menyebut keterbatasan desain single-group.

### Tindakan Revisi

Tambahkan penanda bahwa studi bersifat exploratory dan descriptive.

### Abstrak Versi Lebih Aman

> This study examines how metacognitive awareness may be habituated in primary students' speaking practices through digital microlearning-supported reflective routines. Responding to the tendency of primary speaking instruction to prioritize fluency, accuracy, and teacher-expected responses, the study adopted a classroom-based exploratory intervention design involving 28 Grade 5 students at SDN Mandiri Baros 1-6, Cimahi, Indonesia. The four-week intervention consisted of eight speaking sessions in which concise digital prompts were embedded before, during, and after speaking tasks to cue planning, monitoring, and reflection. Data were collected through an observation rubric, session-level behavioral records, student reflections, and teacher notes, and were analyzed descriptively and thematically. The results indicated increases in observed metacognitive speaking behaviors. Mean scores increased from 1.42 to 2.68 for planning, from 1.36 to 2.51 for monitoring, and from 1.18 to 2.74 for reflection, while overall metacognitive awareness increased from 1.32 to 2.64. Student-session behavioral data also showed increases in pausing to plan, self-correction during speaking, and voluntary reflection after speaking. Qualitative reflections suggested a gradual shift from brief evaluative comments toward more process-oriented awareness of speaking. The findings suggest that repeated reflective routines supported by concise digital prompts may help make metacognitive awareness more observable in primary speaking practices. Given the single-group design and short intervention period, the findings should be interpreted as descriptive evidence of an emerging classroom process rather than causal evidence of intervention effectiveness.

## 17. Masalah Kesimpulan

### Masalah

Kesimpulan masih menyiratkan bahwa digital microlearning menyebabkan peningkatan. Perlu lebih hati-hati.

### Kesimpulan Versi Lebih Aman

> This study suggests that metacognitive awareness in primary students' speaking practices can be supported through repeated reflective routines embedded in digital microlearning prompts. Across the intervention, observed mean scores increased from 1.42 to 2.68 for planning, from 1.36 to 2.51 for monitoring, and from 1.18 to 2.74 for reflection, while the overall metacognitive awareness score increased from 1.32 to 2.64. Session-level behavioral observations also indicated that pausing to plan, self-correction, and voluntary reflection became more frequent in the final sessions than in the early sessions. Qualitative reflections further suggested that students gradually moved from brief affective comments toward more process-oriented statements about what they intended to say, how they monitored their speech, and what they wanted to improve.
>
> These findings should be interpreted as descriptive evidence of an emerging habituation process rather than as causal evidence of intervention effectiveness. The study involved a single intact class, a relatively short intervention period, and no comparison group. Future research should employ longer intervention periods, larger and more diverse samples, comparison-group designs, and more rigorous reliability procedures for observational scoring.

## 19. Apakah Data Terlihat Fishy?

### Jawaban Singkat

Data belum terlihat palsu, tetapi tampak belum cukup diaudit.

### Bagian yang Paling Berisiko Terlihat Fishy

1. **Tabel 2**  
   Angka 51.79% dan 66.07% tidak cocok jika denominator-nya 28 siswa. Ini harus dijelaskan sebagai student-session observations.

2. **Kategori kualitatif 10-10-10-10**  
   Distribusi yang terlalu simetris terlihat kurang natural jika tidak dijelaskan sebagai purposive excerpt sampling.

3. **Perbedaan angka kecil antara bagian hasil dan kesimpulan**  
   Ini membuat data tampak belum final.

4. **Kutipan siswa terlalu generik**  
   Kutipan perlu dikembalikan ke bentuk yang lebih natural atau tampilkan versi bahasa Indonesia dan terjemahannya.

5. **Tidak ada SD atau distribusi skor**  
   Mean saja membuat data sulit diperiksa kewajarannya.

### Cara Membuat Data Terlihat Lebih Kredibel

1. Tambahkan frekuensi mentah.
2. Tambahkan SD atau median.
3. Jelaskan denominator.
4. Jelaskan prosedur scoring.
5. Jelaskan siapa yang menilai.
6. Tambahkan contoh data refleksi asli.
7. Jelaskan proses coding kualitatif.
8. Hindari angka yang terlalu simetris jika itu hanya contoh kutipan, bukan hasil coding frekuentif.
