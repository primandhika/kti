# Informasi penulis dari naskah sumber


Afiliasi telah mengikuti konfirmasi penulis. Biografi berikut disalin dari naskah sumber sebagai arsip pendamping, bukan bagian naskah IJOLE.


Restu Bias Primandhika     is a lecturer expertised in Language Learning, Linguistics and Digital Learning Media. He received his master's degree in Indonesian Language Education of IKIP Siliwangi and is a doctoral students in Universitas Muhammadiyah Prof. Dr. Hamka. In 2018 he joined the Faculty of Language Education of IKIP Siliwangi as lecturer. He has written several papers in the areas of the impact of learning technology on cognitive abilities: exploring digital media preferences of Indonesian language education students, AI’s impact on students’ oral presentation skills: Indonesian lecturers’ pedagogical responses, experiment on a transformer model Indonesian-to-sundanese neural machine translation with Sundanese speech level evaluation. His research interests also include digital learning media, Indonesian language education, learning technology, pedagogical innovations, artificial intelligence in education, machine translation, etc. He can be contacted at email: restubiasprimandhika@uhamka.ac.id.


Nani Solihati     is a professor in Indonesian Language and Literature Education at the Post-Graduate School, Universitas Muhammadiyah Prof. DR. Hamka (UHAMKA). She has researched extensively and has widely published in the areas of critical thinking tasks in language textbooks, literary psychology, gender inequality in literature, and character education through traditional games. She can be contacted at email: nani_solihati@uhamka.ac.id.


Siti Zulaiha     is an Associate Professor, the Head of the Master’s Degree Program in English Education, and a senior lecturer at the Graduate School of Universitas Muhammadiyah Prof. DR. HAMKA (UHAMKA). She conducts research on primary school foreign language learning, teaching, and assessment, implementation of next-generation assessment models, English curriculum development, and the perceptions and pedagogical challenges faced by EFL teachers in language classrooms. These studies have been published in various refereed articles, book chapters, and academic publications, including insights on language assessment and English linguistics.
She can be contacted at email: siti.zulaiha@uhamka.ac.id.


Aurelia Sakti Yani    is a lecturer in Indonesian Language and Literature Education. Aurelia Sakti Yani received both her master's degree (S2) and bachelor's degree (S1) in Indonesian Language and Literature Education from IKIP Siliwangi (formerly STKIP Siliwangi), Cimahi, Indonesia. She joined the Faculty of Language Education at IKIP Siliwangi, Cimahi, Indonesia as a lecturer. She has written several papers in the areas of educational technology optimization, psychological aspects of education, digital literacy, and digital netiquette. Her research interests also include speculative fiction analysis in educational technology, youth delinquency in educational psychology, anti-human trafficking awareness, and community empowerment. She can be contacted at email: saktiyani@ikipsiliwangi.ac.id.
